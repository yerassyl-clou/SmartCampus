# SmartCampus SIS 13 rubric compliance

**Integration audit · 24 November 2026 · Asia/Almaty (UTC+05:00)**

Criteria were transcribed from the course handout *SIS 13. Sprint Retrospective & Continuous Improvement Challenge* (IT Project Management, 5 points) and expanded into evidence checks. The [live delivery guide](https://app.clickup.com/1100310000007240/docs/z8q7hb2y28-918/z8q7hb2y28-158) defines the SmartCampus reporting window and artifact locations.

The six graded parts carry 0.75, 0.75, 1.00, 0.75, 1.25 and 0.50 points respectively. The matrix records evidence coverage for those parts plus submission and release checks. It has no final score or acceptance decision.

A task link identifies responsibility. In the artifact column, a path prefixed with `Expected` is reserved for a missing output. Paths are relative to `docs/SIS13/`. `verified` means the specific requirement was inspected. `partial` means relevant evidence exists with a remaining gap. `missing` means required evidence was not available at this checkpoint. `blocked` means the stated prerequisite is unresolved. `not yet assessed` marks a future cross-artifact review.
