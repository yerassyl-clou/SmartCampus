# Business case

## Position

SmartCampus is a bounded academic project. Current launch assumptions are synthetic and projected; they are not evidence of a real customer agreement.

## Historical projection

28 department-months × $200 = $5,600 revenue. 12 fixed-month units × $160 = $1,920 fixed cost. 28 variable units × $30 = $840 variable cost. $800 startup cost. Net: $2,040. The stated baseline break-even month is M10.

The downside scenario at 12 department-months produces -$680; a 14 department-month case produces -$340 under the stated historical assumption. These are projections, not receipts.

## Current V2 launch

The launch model is $0 billed operations when provider and infrastructure quotas remain verified. Unpaid operator labor, quota exhaustion and unavailable receipts must be monitored. Teams and OCR are future gated options.
