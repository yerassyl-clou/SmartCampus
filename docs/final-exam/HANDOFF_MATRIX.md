N = finish-to-start, consumer waits on producer. I = informational, compatible fixture or document prep can proceed before the relevant acceptance gate. Two forms of timing on the same row distinguish actual preliminary availability from accepted dependency authority. All 82 register pairs are mirrored in both task contracts.

For each row the producer delivers a Markdown envelope at docs/final-exam/handoffs/FINAL-XX.md. Any listed relative document, fixture or evidence path resolves under docs/final-exam unless a contract marks it as repository-root code/config. Record version (v2 or stated fixture version), exact SHA/PR or checksum, occurred\_at ISO, deadline, surface, signed disposition where needed, and preliminary/ready/accepted/blocked status. Notify the named consumer once when ready, accepted, or delayed and their next action changes. Consumer checks exact revision, expected format, stated scenario and authorized environment before relying on the handoff. Use the specific fallback below on delay.
